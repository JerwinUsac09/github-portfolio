# Commercial Bank – Cybersecurity Risk Assessment and Prioritization

## Overview

A cybersecurity risk assessment activity completed as part of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. The exercise involved evaluating potential risks within a commercial bank's operational environment and prioritizing them based on likelihood and severity.

## Objective

Evaluate identified risks affecting the bank's assets, information systems, and data. Assign likelihood and severity scores, calculate risk priority, and determine which risks require greater attention.

## Operational Environment

The simulated bank environment includes:
- 100 on-premises employees and 20 remote employees.
- 2,000 individual customer accounts and 200 commercial accounts.
- External business partnerships and marketing relationships.
- Financial regulatory requirements involving the protection of data and funds.

## Risk Assessment Results

Five potential risks were evaluated:

| Identified Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Business email compromise | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Compromised user database | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| Financial records leak | 3 | 3 | 9 |
| Theft | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Supply chain disruption | 1 | 2 | 2 |

## Risk Scoring Methodology

Risk priority was calculated using the following formula:

**Risk Priority = Likelihood × Severity**

Both likelihood and severity were scored on a scale of 1 to 3.
